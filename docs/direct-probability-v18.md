Direct probability forecasting v18

This separately registered study compares normalized categorical cross-entropy with exact discrete CRPS on the same future-price distribution. The unchanged official export, earlier negative studies, checkpoint selections and prospective protocols remain separate.

The initial audit uses all qualified CC BY 4.0 H100/A100 daily-statistic headline series from the three approved collectors. Training origins run through October 4 with matured labels through October 11, allowing legitimate FastGPU history. Development origins are October 11/18; the descriptive retrospective origins are October 25/28/30, with all labels on or before November 6. These dates remain dependent and previously examined history is not an untouched test.

The level grid uses training outcomes only. Actual outcomes are never clipped. CRPS is computed on positive dollar support; quantiles use the inverse categorical CDF. Both objectives normalize over the same candidate-token probabilities, without decoding JSON or sampling 64 vectors. BLS and news are excluded from the primary contrast.
