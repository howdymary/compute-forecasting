# Pretraining source and renderer amendment

Before any model sampling or optimization, the first provisional data audit revealed a marketplace-ask series selected by its approved collector name. That instrument has an unresolved source-specific redistribution caveat. The study now requires both an approved collector and an approved instrument class, plus the catalog CC BY 4.0 label. Marketplace asks are excluded. The superseded provisional audit artifact and its log are removed; no model result was used to revise this rule.

The numeric renderer is the official qwen3_5_disable_thinking variant. It uses the same non-thinking template for SFT and sampling, preventing a reasoning-token budget from confounding format validity.

The updated protocol is locked before training. Forecast targets and historical evaluation periods remain retrospective. Official submissions and earlier studies are unchanged.
