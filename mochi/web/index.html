All evaluation conditions & limitations ＋

State-based, parameterized controller search. The base is supported; this is upper-body manipulation, not walking or vision-based control. Only the right arm is actuated. Cosmetic expressions do not affect the task. Friction and mass vary; obstacles are not implemented.

Small samples: intervals describe uncertainty, not general robotics capability. Research and random search match simulation budgets; retrieval and model costs are additional. A selected policy can fail on unseen conditions.
