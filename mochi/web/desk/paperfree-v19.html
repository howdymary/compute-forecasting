CHECKPOINT SELECTION

Checkpoint progression

Intermediate versions use a development scenario. Final unseen scenarios compare the frozen parent and the checkpoint selected before testing. Selection prioritizes qualified completion and at least 4.5 mm median moving-sole clearance on every flat leg, then fewer measured steps. One fewer step outweighs all time and arm-travel tie-breakers. The target is four qualified fresh attempts and at least 10% fewer paired steps without longer time. All steps, including alignment, remain in the totals.

FINAL UNSEEN SCENARIOS

Final evaluation

Small simulation pilot. Completion times exclude remote inference waits. No hardware or general-purpose locomotion result is claimed.

WHAT WAS TRAINED

Model and controller boundary

Tinker updates the shared Qwen model that chooses walking strategies, contact profiles and arm recovery from structured simulator state. The camera renders recorded motion. Both sides use the same paper-free scene, bounded command library, physical model and success checks. The safe book controller and native gait weights stay fixed. past.dev carries sourced hypotheses and measured results between research stages; Exa supplies live research and Modal runs the physical experiments.
