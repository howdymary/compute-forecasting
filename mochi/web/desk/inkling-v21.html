MODEL TO EXPERIMENT

Inkling in the research loop

Inkling 2 Mini receives measured failures, relevant controller source and recorded simulation images through Tinker. It proposes bounded changes. A typed interpreter applies permitted parameters; generated source is never executed. MuJoCo determines whether each proposal works.

past.dev carries the prior proposal into the measured review. Exa supplies failure-driven research. Modal hosts the authenticated research backend. Credentials remain server-side.

REJECTED IDEAS

Failures changed the next proposal

Zeroing only terminal translation increased a matched walking leg from 110 to 154 steps. A proposed station pose with 4 mm tracking timed out after 367 alignment attempts. Earlier alignment kept 110 total steps and increased alignment steps. Returning to the existing tolerance was excluded as a no-op.

The revised proposal moves the post-laptop clearance point from y = 0.105 m to 0.055 m before turning. It converted two fresh ankle/laptop collisions into qualified complete routes. The other two cases still failed during laptop closing.

SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS

Controller engineering, measured in physics

This pilot made zero optimizer updates. Inkling's published weights and the native gait stayed fixed. The separate linked study contains the eight actual Qwen selector updates. With no matched qualified pair, a step or arm-travel reduction is undefined. The additional P6 control passed one of two cases and fell on the other.

Restored-state branches inform research but never appear as stitched demonstrations. Each pane here is one uninterrupted from-reset attempt. All physical steps and failed outcomes remain visible.
