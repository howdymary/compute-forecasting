## Hypotheses and exact models

The earlier 3,151-word mandatory-pivot repair pool remained open after native clique search. This experiment tested whether a fractional descendant-packing bound could guide or eliminate that repair problem. It also removed the mandatory pivot, producing a complete 3,176-word compatible universe around the same162 fixed incumbent words. The unforced model retains all64 removed incumbent words and seeks65 repair words, yielding227 in total.

Every candidate uses its SET of distinct length14 descendants. The LP is maximize sum(x), subject to one occupancy inequality per descendant and nonnegative variables. Each word has at least one descendant, so separate upper bounds x<=1 are unnecessary. SciPy1.18.0/HiGHS dual simplex solved each LP in approximately3seconds.

The dual multipliers were rounded upward at scale1,000,000 and any coverage deficits repaired conservatively. Any valid repair is bounded by their mass divided by the scale; integer rounding gives the table.
