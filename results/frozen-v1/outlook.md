# Two- and three-year rental and resale outlook

These are assumption-driven sensitivities, separate from the scored 1/3/7-day forecasts. Rental index 100 is a specified contract's November 6 quote; resale index 100 is a hypothetical starting value, not an observed transaction. No probabilities or confidence levels are estimated.

| Scenario | Years | Rental index | Resale index |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| supply_expansion | 2 | 42.25 | 36.0 |
| supply_expansion | 3 | 27.46 | 21.6 |
| moderate_decline | 2 | 72.25 | 56.25 |
| moderate_decline | 3 | 61.41 | 42.19 |
| persistent_constraints | 2 | 110.25 | 81.0 |
| persistent_constraints | 3 | 115.76 | 72.9 |

- supply_expansion: rental annual change -0.35; resale annual depreciation 0.4. Rapid capacity addition and substitution toward newer accelerators.
- moderate_decline: rental annual change -0.15; resale annual depreciation 0.25. Continued efficiency gains with continued demand for older accelerators.
- persistent_constraints: rental annual change 0.05; resale annual depreciation 0.1. Capacity constraints support nominal rental rates and slow depreciation.

The envelope is not a calibrated prediction interval. SKU discontinuation, utilization, energy costs, hardware condition and transaction liquidity can dominate these assumptions. There is no open executed resale-price series in the pack.
